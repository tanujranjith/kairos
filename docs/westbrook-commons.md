# Westbrook Commons

Westbrook Commons replaces an unbroken lawn inside the existing downtown blocks with one authored, cell-owned civic park. The compact 38 × 64 m footprint contains terrain-following concrete walks, a shallow fountain and water surface, four benches, four visual path lamps, four planted beds, ten reused oak/trunk pairs and twelve grass-card shrubs. All hardscape and furniture append to the existing wall, roof and glass batches; planting uses the existing thin-instance libraries. No new material, texture, light, runtime host or draw-call family is introduced.

The park footprint participates in `urbanReserved`, so procedural buildings cannot overlap it. Its four walk arms remain open, its corners clear every road edge by more than 80 m and every junction envelope by more than 87 m, and the whole authored footprint belongs to cell `-5,-5`. Concrete paths join the collision-bearing structure batch and return four `Concrete` wheel contacts instead of disguising grass with visual-only paving.

An initial 80 × 60 m Orchard Commons trial looked isolated and its neighborhood submitted 599,155 triangles on Low, so it was rejected. The final downtown location integrates with surrounding buildings, uses 980 authored triangles, ten oak instances and twelve grass instances, and displaces overlapping procedural scatter. Installed Edge screenshots were opened under forced WebGL2 and actual WebGPU; both show matching fountain, paths, trees, beds, benches and lamps. Normal keyboard driving on Cedar Street reaches 19.32 m/s with four contacts and zero damage.

The final Low audit records 190 draws / 454,013 triangles at the Commons, 262 / 494,803 in city traffic and 263 / 494,970 in wet night. The shared-car race grid remains the overall narrow case at 291 / 496,870. Three city/forest/lake loops repeat at 907/84/44, 368/85/45 and 319/84/44 meshes/materials/textures; three race/home loops return to 138/83/43 and zero cells. The full suite passes 239 tests in 37 files, strict TypeScript and the production build pass, and cold 25Mbps/40ms localhost delivery reaches the menu in 5.502s WebGL2 / 4.942s automatic while transferring 10,517,845 bytes with no page, failed or external requests.

The generic skill input client and the repository copy each remained live without producing an artifact and were terminated rather than counted as passes. The dedicated stateful browser verifier covers normal keyboard driving, physical park contacts, streaming readiness, screenshots and errors. This checkpoint adds one composed urban open space; it does not add pedestrians, animated fountain spray, a complete city park network, target-laptop certification or hosted HTTPS acceptance.
