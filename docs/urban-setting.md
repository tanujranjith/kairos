# Westbrook urban blocks

Westbrook now has four authored blocks inside its existing street grid: Cedar Square, Market Court, Harbor Exchange and Westbrook Campus. Each block combines one fully articulated hero building with lower-cost band-window secondary wings, a terrain-following paved public realm, planted beds, trees, rooftop equipment, canopies, inlays and restrained parking detail. The four compositions use different massing and heights instead of repeating one isolated tower on uninterrupted grass.

`src/content/urban-setting.ts` owns the fixed footprints. `src/world/urban-setting.ts` generates the original geometry and assigns each block to one 256m cell. The procedural city scatter yields inside each authored footprint, preventing trees or random buildings from intersecting the compositions. Walls, roofs, windows and inlays merge into the existing per-cell material buffers; only the four occupied cells gain one small inlay mesh. No texture, material, light or external asset is added.

The plaza surface uses an eight-metre terrain-conforming grid raised by 0.09m, matching the industrial-yard solution. It participates in the normal structure collider and reports `Concrete` at all four wheels. Building bases sample the terrain at their own centers. Site corners are tested against every road and junction sightline.

The first campus location failed the road-clearance test because Foundry Avenue crosses the north-city block; it was moved to the genuinely clear northwest parcel. The first full composition contained 83,982 authored triangles. That was rejected before live integration. Retaining one detailed hero building per site and replacing secondary wings with band-window architecture reduced it to 37,590 triangles before procedural buildings displaced by the footprints are subtracted. The final live Low city sample is 211 draw calls / 491,399 active triangles, leaving only modest headroom under the specified ceiling.

Screenshot review also rejected exact wall/glass coplanarity on the new continuous bands after WebGPU produced black depth streaks. Their panes now sit a few centimeters proud of the wall and render cleanly on both native paths. A broader architectural-glass material experiment made WebGL2 worse and was reverted. Existing procedural individual-window reflections remain stylized and are not claimed as a global glazing overhaul.

Installed Edge verifies all four block overviews, terrain-following concrete contacts and normal keyboard driving in forced WebGL2 and actual WebGPU. Repeated streaming and race/home cleanup, the complete test suite, strict production build and local cold-cache delivery smoke also pass. This is a substantial city-composition pass, not a photoreal populated city: no pedestrians, parked-car system, interior rooms or target-laptop/authorized-HTTPS certification are claimed.
